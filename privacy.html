Security & Data Ethics

Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: March 2026

1. The Zero-Knowledge Principle

At LedgerPulse Private Advisory, we adhere to a strict Zero-Knowledge operational protocol regarding our clients' cryptographic keys. Under no circumstances will LedgerPulse, its partners, CPAs, or contractors request, accept, or handle private keys, seed phrases, or exchange login passwords.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide forensic cost-basis reconciliation and tax preparation services, we only collect:

  • Public blockchain wallet addresses (e.g., Ethereum 0x addresses, Solana public keys).
  • Read-only CSV trade exports and read-only API access tokens where granted.
  • Basic identifying information required under tax practitioner regulations (CPA Circular 230 / CRA standards).

3. Encrypted Storage & Retention

All client files, reconciliation workbooks, and tax return packages are encrypted using AES-256 standards both in transit and at rest. Access is limited solely to the primary advisor assigned to your engagement.

4. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing

We never monetize, sell, or distribute client data to third parties, analytics brokers, or marketing networks. Information is only submitted to tax authorities (IRS, CRA) upon direct authorization by you as the taxpayer.
