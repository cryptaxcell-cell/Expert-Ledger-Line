Professional Standards

Terms of Advisory Engagement

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: March 2026

1. Scope of Advisory Services

LedgerPulse provides digital-asset tax preparation, cost-basis normalization, bookkeeping, and consulting. Each engagement is governed by a formalized Statement of Work (SOW) detailing the specific tax years, entities, and deliverables requested.

2. Taxpayer Responsibility & Documentation

The taxpayer is responsible for the completeness and authenticity of all transaction records provided. While LedgerPulse applies forensic verification methods, clients must confirm that all wallets and exchange accounts held during the taxable period have been disclosed.

3. Audit Defense & Representation

Returns prepared by LedgerPulse are backed by full workpaper documentation. Formal representation before the IRS or CRA in an examination requires separate power of attorney (IRS Form 2848 / CRA T1013) and will be conducted under our audit defense protocol.
